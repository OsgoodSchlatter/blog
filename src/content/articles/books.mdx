A running log of books I've read over the years — fiction, essays, comics,
the occasional physics textbook. Notes are quick impressions, not full
reviews. I'll keep adding to this one as I go.

| Cover | Year | Title | Author | Notes |
|-------|------|-------|--------|-------|
| ![L'Attentat cover](/images/books/lattentat.jpg) | 2025 | **L'Attentat** | Yasmina Khadra | Set in Israel, this book follows an Arab surgeon who witnesses a terrorist attack — only to discover the terrorist was his wife. What led her to do so? A great story about the difficulty of being integrated as an Arab in Israel. |
| ![Le Procès cover](/images/books/kafka.jpg) | 2024 | **Le Procès** | Kafka | A book that takes you through an imaginary and helpless trial and fills you with frustration. |
| ![Siddharta cover](/images/books/siddharta.jpg) | 2024 | **Siddharta** | Hermann Hesse | A book that guides through different lives embodied by the young Siddharta, who is seeking Nirvana. |
| ![Neutron Physics cover](/images/books/Neutron_P.jpg) | 2024 | **Neutron Physics** | Paul Reuss | Physics of neutrons in a nuclear reactor. |
| ![Histoire de Jérusalem cover](/images/books/jerusalem.jpeg) | 2024 | **Histoire de Jérusalem** | Vincent Lemire - Christophe Gaultier | A comic that retraces the story of Jerusalem from 2000 BCE to now. Good reading to understand today's issues in the Middle East. |
| ![La coscienza di Zeno (La conscience de Zeno) cover](/images/books/zeno.jpg) | 2024 | **La coscienza di Zeno (La conscience de Zeno)** | Italo Svevo (Ettore Schmitz) | A kind of autobiographical story of a businessman from Trieste who tries to ponder his life and his acts. |
| ![L'archipel d'une autre vie cover](/images/books/andrei.jpeg) | 2023 | **L'archipel d'une autre vie** | Andreï Makine | A Russian soldier is sent into the depths of Russia and tries to escape the army. Beautiful sceneries. |
| ![1984 cover](/images/books/1984.jpeg) | 2022 | **1984** | George Orwell | This book depicts a society in which an entity called Big Brother watches over every move you make — and beyond moves, analyzes every thought you have. History is rewritten, and some words become forbidden. |
| ![Plan de Transformation de l'Economie Française cover](/images/books/ptef.jpeg) | 2020 | **Plan de Transformation de l'Economie Française** | The Shift Project | A book that explains how we can slowly (but steadily) re-plan and shift our economy towards a more sustainable one. |
| ![L'Assemblée des Femmes cover](/images/books/assemblee.jpg) | 2020 | **L'Assemblée des Femmes** | Aristophane | This play depicts a city that tries to change its social norms. A group of women takes power and enforces new rules, notably a share of all goods and the right for the least sought-after women to choose a partner. |
| ![Songe d'une nuit d'été (A Midsummer Night's Dream) cover](/images/books/midsummer-nights-dream.webp) | 2020 | **Songe d'une nuit d'été (A Midsummer Night's Dream)** | Shakespeare | Confusion and love in ancient Greece. |
| ![Le Banquet cover](/images/books/Le-Banquet.jpg) | 2020 | **Le Banquet** | Platon | Several guests explore the nature of Love during a feast. |
| ![The Plot Against America cover](/images/books/complot_roth.jpg) | 2020 | **The Plot Against America** | Philip Roth | This book depicts the rise of fascism and antisemitism in 1940s America, in an alternate history where Charles Lindbergh becomes president. |
| ![Bonjour Tristesse cover](/images/books/bonjour_tristesse.jpeg) | 2018 | **Bonjour Tristesse** | Françoise Sagan | Cécile, her father Raymond, and his mistress Elsa are on vacation in the South of France. Cécile likes spending time with Elsa, but Raymond suddenly brings Anne home. Her seriousness and discipline impact the trio, and her influence makes Raymond leave Elsa for her. But things are not so simple. |
| ![Le joueur d'échecs cover](/images/books/zweig.jpg) | 2016 | **Le joueur d'échecs** | Stefan Zweig | A man has played chess in his head for several years due to an arrest, and tries to play against the world champion. |
| ![Se questo è un uomo (Si c'est un homme) cover](/images/books/primo_levi.jpeg) | 2014 | **Se questo è un uomo (Si c'est un homme)** | Primo Levi | Primo Levi enlightens us on what life is like in concentration camps. |
| ![À l'ouest rien de nouveau cover](/images/books/remarque.jpg) | 2013 | **À l'ouest rien de nouveau** | Erich Maria Remarque | Erich Maria Remarque enlightens us on what life is like in the trenches during WWI. |
