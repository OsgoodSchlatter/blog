In this post, I want to share my own creation of an $H_2$ generator.

## What is the objective?

The objective is to create $H_2$ from $H_2O$ and electrical current.

This is done by placing two conductive materials in a mixture of water and
another component called the **electrolyte**. We then run an electric current
through the conductive parts, which are connected by the water.

## How does it work?

Two main reactions are taken into account. The first occurs at one electrode,
while the other occurs at the other electrode.

One electrode is called the **cathode**, where the following reaction occurs:

$$
2H_2O + 2e^- \rightarrow H_2 + 2OH^-
$$

This reaction is called the **reduction of $H_2O$**. It consumes electrons and
releases $H_2$.

The other electrode is called the **anode**, where the following reaction occurs:

$$
4OH^- \rightarrow O_2 + 2H_2O + 4e^-
$$

This reaction is called the **oxidation of $H_2O$**. It releases electrons and
produces $O_2$.

The $OH^-$ ions act as the electrolyte. They are introduced into the solution
using $NaOH$, although $KOH$ can also be used.

The solution is therefore **alkaline**. Similar reactions can also occur in
acidic solutions, but different electrodes and electrolytes are required.
